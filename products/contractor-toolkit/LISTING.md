## Description
Stop losing money on jobs you never tracked. This all-in-one workbook gives contractors and tradespeople everything to quote, bill and measure profit, no accounting software needed.

**What's inside**
- **Estimate**: professional, printable estimate with labor/materials lines, automatic sales tax on materials, and a deposit calculation
- **Invoice**: same layout with amount paid and balance due
- **Job Tracker**: revenue, labor, materials and other costs per job, with automatic profit and margin, color-coded status
- **Expense Log**: 500 rows with category drop-downs
- **Summary dashboard**: revenue, profit, margin, outstanding balances and expenses by category with a chart
- **Settings**: enter your business details and tax rate once and every tab updates

**Works with:** Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets (upload the file to Drive and open with Sheets). Instant digital download; nothing is shipped.

**Good for:** plumbers, HVAC, electricians, handymen, painters, landscapers, cleaners, remodelers, and any solo or small-crew service business.

**Please note:** This is a spreadsheet template, not tax or accounting advice. Sample data is included so you can see how it works; clear the yellow cells to start fresh.
